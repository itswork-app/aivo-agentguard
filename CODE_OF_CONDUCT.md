# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, factual, and constructive.
- Welcome good-faith questions and differing technical opinions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intimidation, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. This initial policy is intentionally lightweight and may be expanded as the project grows.
